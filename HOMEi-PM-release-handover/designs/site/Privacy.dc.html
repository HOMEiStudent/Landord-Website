Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026 · HOMEi, Sheffield, United Kingdom

This policy explains what personal data HOMEi ("we", "us") collects through the HOMEi PM website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it deliberately short and plain. If anything is unclear, email us at support@homeistudent.com.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details. When you contact us through the website we collect your name, email address, how you manage your properties, and anything you choose to tell us in the optional message field.
  • Correspondence. If you email us, we keep the email and our reply.
  • Anonymous analytics. With your consent, we collect anonymous page statistics to understand how the site is used. No identifiable data.

The savings calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter into it is sent to us or stored by us.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to contact you about HOMEi PM: getting set up, onboarding, and new landlord guides. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for unrelated marketing.

Legal basis

We process enquiry details on the basis of your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing support@homeistudent.com and we will delete your details.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, or until you ask us to delete them. Account data you create inside the HOMEi PM platform is covered by the terms you agree to when you sign up.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and to object to or restrict our processing of it. To exercise any of these rights, email support@homeistudent.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
