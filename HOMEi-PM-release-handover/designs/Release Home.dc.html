Renters' Rights Act 2025

What the regulations now ask of you,
and where HOMEi PM steps in

Section 21 is gone and paperwork has replaced it.
HOMEi PM is built from the ground up around these rules for landlords in England.

The ruleWhere it goes wrongWhat you no longer worry about
The ruleOne rent increase a year, with two months' notice.
Where it goes wrongOne error invalidates the increase, and your rent falls behind the market.
What you no longer worry aboutSection 13 rent increase trackerEvery notice and deadline tracked for you. No more second-guessing the dates.
The ruleTenants must receive their written terms and required documents.
Where it goes wrongMissing written information carries a penalty starting at £4,000, up to £7,000.
What you no longer worry aboutNotice and document vaultDocuments delivered to tenants, with receipts stored. The proof is there if you are ever asked for it.
The rulePossession only through Section 8, backed by evidence.
Where it goes wrongA notice outside the Section 8 process carries a penalty starting at £6,000.
What you no longer worry aboutSection 8 evidence packsEvery repair and message stored with timestamps. Your records are ready before you need them.

How a repair request works
  1. 1Your tenant reports the issue in the HOMEi app, with photos and a description.
  2. 2You review the request from your dashboard and decide what action it needs, such as arranging a contractor.
  3. 3You send your tenant a time slot to accept, so you avoid paying for a wasted contractor visit.
  4. 4Your tenant follows progress in the app from reported to resolved, and the whole trail is stored as evidence.
