Renters' Rights Act 2025

The Act's biggest obligations, tracked in one place

Section 21 is gone. What replaces it is paperwork, and the landlords who keep clean records will be the ones who protect their rental income and their returns.

SECTION 13Rent increase trackerOne increase per year, two months' written notice. An error invalidates it and your rent quietly falls behind the market. We track every notice and deadline for you.
SECTION 8Evidence pack builderEvery inspection, repair and message stored with timestamps: a court-ready audit trail that avoids costly delays when you need possession.
DOCUMENTSNotice and document vaultGas safety, EPC, deposit and information sheets delivered to tenants with receipts stored, so a missed document never turns into a fine.
Connected to the HOMEi app

Your tenants use their app. You manage everything from your browser.

HOMEi PM is a web-based dashboard: nothing to install, log in from your laptop, tablet or phone. Your tenants use the HOMEi household management app ↗, and two separate tools connect them to you. Inspections: when you request one, tenants complete a guided photo checklist that becomes a full condition report. Maintenance: tenants report issues with photos and track the progress of their submission from reported to resolved.

1.Tenant reports a leak with photos in the HOMEi app
2.You decide whether the maintenance needs action, from your dashboard
3.The fix is actioned, and the whole trail is stored as evidence
Early-reported maintenance is the single biggest saving. A leak caught at report stage is a small repair; left unseen it becomes an insurance claim, an excess and years of higher premiums. See how we work this out
