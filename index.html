Your tenants complete self-inspections through HOMEi, the Shared Household Organiser. They photograph each room and note any issues, so you get the data you need without arranging a visit.

Why Our Inspection System Works

A Better Way to Inspect Your Properties

Traditional inspections cost you time and money. Our system uses your tenants to build a complete picture of each property, without the hassle.

The Old Way

  • Schedule visits around tenants' availability
  • Travel to each property in person
  • One inspector's view of the property
  • Paper forms or manual spreadsheets
  • Costs add up fast across multiple properties
  • Intrusive for tenants who need to be home

The HOMEi Way

  • Tenants complete inspections on their own time
  • No travel needed, inspections come to you
  • Multiple tenants contribute to a full property picture
  • Digital records with photos, stored automatically
  • Lower cost per property, scales easily
  • Less intrusive, tenants report at their convenience

Lower Cost

No site visits means no travel expenses or inspector fees. Run inspections across your whole portfolio without the overhead.

Saves Time

No scheduling, no driving between properties. Tenants submit inspections at a convenient time for them and you, and you review the results from your desk.

More Accurate

Individual reports from each tenant are combined to build a full, accurate overview of the property condition rather than relying on one person's assessment.

Renters' Rights Act 2025

Built for the Renters' Rights Act

The biggest change to UK lettings law in decades. Here's how we're building HOMEi to keep landlords on the right side of it.

Coming Soon

Section 13 Rent Increase Tracker

Rent can now only increase once per year with 2 months' written notice — errors invalidate the increase entirely. HOMEi will generate, store and track Section 13 notices with automated reminders.

Coming Soon

Section 8 Evidence Pack Builder

With Section 21 abolished, possessions must use Section 8 with documented legal grounds. HOMEi stores every inspection, maintenance request and message with timestamps for court-ready audit trails.

Coming Soon

Section 4A Compliance Tracker

Student landlords must serve notice between 1 May and 30 July with 4 months' notice — miss the window and you wait a full year. HOMEi will alert you when the window opens and track deadlines.

Coming Soon

Termination Notice Storage

Termination notices must now be issued in writing or email and stored as legal records. HOMEi will automatically create a permanent, timestamped record of every notice.

Coming Soon

Prescribed Information Delivery

Landlords must provide tenants with the Government Information Sheet — failure risks fines of up to £7,000. HOMEi will send, track and store delivery confirmation automatically.
