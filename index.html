Platform Features

Everything You Need to Manage Properties Efficiently

HOMEi combines powerful tools into one seamless platform, designed specifically for landlords and estate agents who want to work smarter.

Cost-Effective Inspections

Reduce inspection costs by empowering tenants to complete guided self-inspections from their smartphones. Get detailed photo evidence and condition reports without expensive site visits.

Seamless Communication

Built-in messaging and smart notifications keep landlords, agents, and tenants connected. No more chasing emails or missed phone calls — everything in one thread.

Digital Property Records

Maintain comprehensive digital records of every property, inspection, and communication. Access your complete property history anytime, from anywhere.

Maintenance Tracking

Tenants report issues instantly with photos and descriptions. Track every maintenance request from submission to resolution with full audit trails.

Compliance & Reporting

Generate professional, audit-ready reports for compliance, inventory checks, and condition assessments. Stay ahead of regulations with automated documentation.

Portfolio Dashboard

Manage your entire property portfolio from a single, intuitive dashboard. Get real-time visibility across all properties, tenants, and upcoming tasks.
