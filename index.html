Stay compliant with the Renters' Rights Act 2026

The Renters' Rights Act changes everything. Section 21 no-fault evictions are gone. Periodic tenancies become the default. Section 13 rent increase rules are stricter. HOMEi PM is being built with these changes at its core.

  • Track Section 13 rent increase deadlines
  • Store inspection evidence for Section 8 proceedings
  • Maintain timestamped communication records
  • Monitor compliance deadlines across your portfolio

HOMEi is a shared household organiser for student tenants — it helps housemates manage bills, chores, house rules and more. HOMEi PM is the landlord-facing side of the same ecosystem. When your tenants already use HOMEi, completing inspections and reporting maintenance through HOMEi PM feels natural rather than like yet another platform to learn.

You set up an inspection request from your dashboard. Tenants receive a notification and complete a structured checklist on their phone — room by room, with photos at each stage. The completed report with timestamped photos lands in your dashboard automatically. You review, approve or follow up, all without leaving your desk.

Tenants submit maintenance issues directly through HOMEi PM (or the connected HOMEi app). Each issue is logged with a description and optional photos. You can update the status, add notes and mark issues as resolved — building a full audit trail for every property over time.

No. HOMEi PM is entirely web-based. You access your dashboard through any browser on any device — desktop, tablet or phone. Nothing to download, nothing to install, nothing to update manually.

The initial focus is student and HMO landlords in the UK, where the connection to the HOMEi tenant app is most relevant. However, the core features — inspections, maintenance tracking, messaging and compliance tools — are useful for any UK private landlord regardless of tenant type.
