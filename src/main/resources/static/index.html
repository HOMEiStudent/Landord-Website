What HOMEi Property Management Does

We handle the two things that take up most of your time as a landlord or agent: inspections and tenant communication.

Guided Property Inspections

Your tenants complete self-inspections through the HOMEi app on their phone. They photograph each room and note any issues, so you get the inspection data you need without arranging a site visit.

  • Tenants submit inspections via the HOMEi app
  • Room-by-room guided photo capture
  • Inspection data sent directly to your dashboard

Inspection Interface Screenshot

Landlord-Tenant Communication

Message your tenants directly through the platform instead of juggling emails and phone calls. All your property conversations are kept together so you can find them when you need them.

  • Direct messaging between landlords and tenants
  • All conversations stored in one place
  • Linked to the tenant's HOMEi app

Web-Based Property Dashboard

Log in from any browser to see your properties, recent inspections, and messages. There's nothing to install and it works on your laptop, tablet, or phone.

  • Works in any modern web browser
  • View inspection history and communication logs
  • Manage multiple properties from a single view
