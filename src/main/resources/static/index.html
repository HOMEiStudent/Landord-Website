Platform Features

Everything You Need in One Place

Inspections, maintenance tracking and tenant communication, all managed from your browser. Here's what the platform does.

Guided Property Inspections

Your tenants complete self-inspections through HOMEi, the Shared Household Organiser. They photograph each room and note any issues, so you get the data you need without arranging a visit.

  • Tenants submit inspections from their phone
  • Room-by-room guided photo capture
  • Results sent straight to your dashboard

Landlord-Tenant Messaging

Message your tenants directly through the platform instead of juggling emails and phone calls. All your property conversations stay in one place so you can find them when you need them.

  • Direct messaging between you and your tenants
  • Full conversation history for every property
  • Connected to the HOMEi household organiser app

Maintenance Reporting & Tracking

Tenants report maintenance issues through the HOMEi household organiser app, complete with photos and descriptions. You receive the request on your dashboard and can track the progress of each job from start to finish.

  • Tenants submit issues with photos and descriptions
  • Track repair progress from reported to resolved
  • Full history of every maintenance request per property

Web-Based Property Dashboard

Log in from any browser to see your properties, inspections, maintenance requests and messages. Nothing to install, and it works on your laptop, tablet or phone.

  • Works in any modern web browser
  • Overview of all your properties in one place
  • Inspection, maintenance and communication history

Why Our Inspection System Works

A Better Way to Inspect Your Properties

Traditional inspections cost you time and money. Our system uses your tenants to build a complete picture of each property, without the hassle.

The Old Way

  • Schedule visits around tenants' availability
  • Travel to each property in person
  • One inspector's view of the property
  • Paper forms or manual spreadsheets
  • Costs add up fast across multiple properties
  • Intrusive for tenants who need to be home

The HOMEi Way

  • Tenants complete inspections on their own time
  • No travel needed, inspections come to you
  • Multiple tenants contribute to a full property picture
  • Digital records with photos, stored automatically
  • Lower cost per property, scales easily
  • Less intrusive, tenants report at their convenience

Lower Cost

No site visits means no travel expenses or inspector fees. Run inspections across your whole portfolio without the overhead.

Saves Time

No scheduling, no driving between properties. Tenants submit inspections when it suits them and you review the results from your desk.

More Accurate

Individual reports from each tenant are combined to build a full, accurate overview of the property condition rather than relying on one person's assessment.
